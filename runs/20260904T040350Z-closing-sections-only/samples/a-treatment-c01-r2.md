`confirmation_text` was reporting the wrong date for evening bookings east of UTC. `booking_day` took the UTC datetime straight from the database and called `strftime` on it, so a Sydney booking on the evening of 11 March came back as 2026-03-11 in the confirmation while the venue considered it the 12th; the failing test in `tests/test_confirm.py` was exactly that case. The two other places that format a booking day, `email.py:47` and `report.py:19`, were already converting through `venue.tz` first, so the bug was confined to the confirmation path.

I gave `booking_day` a `tz` parameter and made it convert before formatting (`src/booking/confirm.py:22`), then updated the one caller to pass `booking.venue.tz` (`src/booking/confirm.py:27`). A grep across `src/` confirms those are the only two references to the function, so no other call site needed the new argument. The full `tests/test_confirm.py` suite passes, 12 tests.
