The suggestive part: of 147 `session expired` events in August, 56 fall in the 09:00 hour on 2026-08-07, 08-14, 08-21 and 08-28. Those are the four Fridays in August, and `deploy/cron.d/rotate-session-keys` runs `0 9 * * 5` — 09:00 every Friday. That is not a coincidence I can talk myself out of.

But `rotate_key()` in `src/auth/keys.py:31` keeps the outgoing key as `previous`, with a comment saying existing sessions keep working, so on its face rotation should be harmless. I never read the verification side to check whether that promise is kept: I did not find or read the code that validates an incoming session token, so I don't know whether it actually falls back to `previous` when `current` fails. If it doesn't, the comment is aspirational and rotation invalidates everything every Friday — but then I would expect far more than 19 sign-outs an hour, so that simple version probably isn't it either.

The mechanism I find most plausible, and did not verify, is double rotation. `SESSION_LIFETIME` is 14 days (`src/auth/config.py:8`) and rotation is weekly, so a session in its second week was signed with a key that is now two rotations old and has been overwritten in the store. Only sessions in that older band would break, which would explain why this hits a minority rather than everyone, and why it lands on Fridays at nine. Confirming it means reading the validation path and checking whether the log lines carry a session-issued timestamp or user ID.

The larger gap is that this explains 56 events at most. Another 9 sit at 2026-08-14T13:00, nowhere near the cron, and roughly 82 are scattered outside the top five buckets entirely. Either there is a second cause, or the rotation story is wrong and something else produces a Friday-morning peak.
