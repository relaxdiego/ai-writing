`booking_day` in `src/booking/confirm.py` was formatting the database timestamp straight from UTC, so the confirmation text reported the UTC calendar date instead of the venue's. The failing test books a Sydney evening slot, where UTC is still on 2026-03-11 while Sydney has rolled over to 2026-03-12, and the customer was being told the wrong day. The two other places that render a booking date, `src/booking/email.py:47` and `src/booking/report.py:19`, already convert with `.astimezone(venue.tz)` before formatting, so `confirm.py` was the outlier rather than the house style.

The fix follows those two: `booking_day` now takes a `tz` argument and converts before formatting (`src/booking/confirm.py:22`), and `confirmation_text` passes `booking.venue.tz` (`src/booking/confirm.py:27`). The comment noting that `when` arrives from the database in UTC is still accurate and I left it in place.

`tests/test_confirm.py` passes, twelve tests including the Sydney case that was red. Two gaps worth knowing about: I ran only that file, not the whole suite, and my search for other callers covered `src/` alone. Since the signature change is breaking for anyone calling `booking_day` with one argument, a caller under `tests/` or elsewhere in the repo would fail on the new required parameter.
