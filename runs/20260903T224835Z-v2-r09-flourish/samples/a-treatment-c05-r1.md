The August log has 147 "session expired" events. Grouping them by hour, the five largest buckets are 2026-08-14T09, 2026-08-21T09, 2026-08-07T09 and 2026-08-28T09, plus one at 2026-08-14T13. Those four dates are the Fridays in August, and `deploy/cron.d/rotate-session-keys` runs `0 9 * * 5`, which is Friday at 09:00. The sign-outs line up with the weekly session-key rotation.

The problem is that `rotate_key()` in `src/auth/keys.py:31` appears to be written specifically to prevent this. It reads the current key, generates a new one, writes the new key to `current`, and moves the old one to `previous`, with a comment saying old sessions keep working. If the verification path checks `previous` on a `current` miss, rotation should be invisible to logged-in users. I did not read the verification path, so I cannot tell you whether it consults `previous` at all, whether it does so only for certain token shapes, or whether something else overwrites `previous` between rotations. That is the first thing to look at, and until someone does, the Friday-09:00 pattern is an association and not a mechanism.
