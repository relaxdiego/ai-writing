`booking_day` in `src/booking/confirm.py` was formatting the UTC datetime straight out of the database, so a booking made in the evening at a venue east of UTC printed the previous day. That is what `tests/test_confirm.py::test_evening_booking_in_sydney` was catching: it expected `2026-03-12` and got `2026-03-11`. The two other places that render a booking day, `email.py:47` and `report.py:19`, both convert to the venue's timezone first, so `confirm.py` was the odd one out rather than the codebase having an unsettled convention.

I gave `booking_day` a `tz` parameter and made it call `astimezone(tz)` before formatting, then updated the one caller, `confirmation_text`, to pass `booking.venue.tz`. A grep for `booking_day` across `src/` confirms those are the only two occurrences, so nothing else needed touching. The full `tests/test_confirm.py` run is green at 12 passed.

The comment above the function saying `when` arrives from the database in UTC is still there and still true; it describes the input, and the conversion now happens after it. I did not run the suite beyond `test_confirm.py`, so `email.py` and `report.py` are unverified by this run, though neither was changed.
