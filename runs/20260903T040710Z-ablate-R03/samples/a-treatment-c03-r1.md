Four of the five files that referenced `LegacyConnection` now use `ConnectionPool`: `src/billing/invoices.py`, `src/billing/refunds.py`, `src/reports/monthly.py` and `src/reports/export.py`. The test suite passes after the change, 29 passed with 3 skipped; I did not look into what the skipped tests cover, so I can't say whether any of them exercise the paths I touched.

The fifth file, `src/admin/backfill.py`, I left on `LegacyConnection`, and it is the one thing standing between you and a clean sweep. The backfill streams roughly 40M rows through `LegacyConnection.raw_cursor()` in chunks of 10,000, and a comment at line 40 records that `ConnectionPool` had no streaming cursor equivalent as of v3.2. Rewriting it against the pool's ordinary cursor would materialise the whole result set, so the migration there is not a mechanical substitution: it needs either a streaming API on `ConnectionPool` or a keyset-paginated rewrite of the query, both of which change the code's behaviour under `repeatable_read` in ways worth deciding deliberately. A `grep` for `LegacyConnection` in `src/` now returns that file alone.
