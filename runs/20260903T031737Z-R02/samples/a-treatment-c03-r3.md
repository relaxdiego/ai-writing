I migrated four of the five files off `LegacyConnection` — `src/billing/invoices.py`, `src/billing/refunds.py`, `src/reports/monthly.py`, and `src/reports/export.py` all now use `ConnectionPool`. The full test suite passes: 29 passed, 3 skipped.

The fifth file, `src/admin/backfill.py`, I left on `LegacyConnection`. It depends on `raw_cursor()` to stream 40M rows in 10k chunks without materialising them, and per the note in the file, `ConnectionPool` has no streaming cursor equivalent as of v3.2. Migrating it would mean either loading the whole result set into memory or hand-rolling a pagination scheme with its own correctness questions around the `repeatable_read` isolation the backfill currently relies on, so I stopped rather than guess at which tradeoff you wanted. That leaves the deprecation incomplete: a `grep` for `LegacyConnection` in `src/` still returns `src/admin/backfill.py`.

Two ways forward, depending on what's available to you. If a newer `ConnectionPool` ships a streaming cursor, the migration is probably mechanical and I can do it once you confirm the version. Otherwise the backfill needs a deliberate redesign — keyset pagination over an indexed column is the usual replacement — which is a larger change than this task implied and worth scoping separately.
