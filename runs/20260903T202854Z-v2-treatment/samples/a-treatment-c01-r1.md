`booking_day` in `src/booking/confirm.py` was formatting the raw UTC timestamp it got from the database, so a booking late enough in the evening at a venue ahead of UTC came out on the previous calendar day. That's what `test_evening_booking_in_sydney` was catching: it expected `2026-03-12` and got `2026-03-11`. The function now takes the venue timezone and converts before formatting, and `confirmation_text` passes `booking.venue.tz` through. `tests/test_confirm.py` passes, 12 of 12.

Grepping for the same pattern across `src/booking/` turned up two other places that format a booking day, `email.py:47` and `report.py:19`, and both already called `astimezone(venue.tz)` first. Confirmation was the odd one out rather than one instance of a general problem, which is why the fix stayed local. The signature change to `booking_day` is safe on the same evidence: a grep for the name across `src/` finds only the definition and the single call site I updated.
